BrightBean Social Studio is a content-planning and publishing workspace. Its YouTube integration lets an authorized channel owner or manager prepare, schedule, and upload videos and Shorts, manage comments, and review channel and video analytics from one workspace.

What the integration does

  • Connects the YouTube channel selected by the user through Google's OAuth consent flow.
  • Uploads a video or Short only after the user selects the channel, media, title, description, and visibility.
  • Supports public, unlisted, and private visibility chosen by the user before publication.
  • Shows publishing status and the YouTube video ID for the completed upload.
  • Provides authorized comment-management and analytics features when enabled.

User control

The user initiates each connection and publishing action. BrightBean Studio does not request a Google password and does not change YouTube visibility without the user's selection. Access can be revoked by disconnecting the channel in BrightBean Studio or from Google's third-party connections settings.

Data and policies

Our Privacy Policy explains the Google and YouTube data we access, why we use it, retention periods, Limited Use compliance, and deletion controls. Please also review our Terms of Service and Data Deletion Instructions.

Support

Questions about the YouTube integration can be sent to admin@shopauth.cloud. Existing users can sign in to BrightBean Studio to connect or manage a channel.
