You can disconnect a social account at any time and can request deletion of your BrightBean Studio account and associated personal data. You do not need to provide your Instagram, Facebook, Google, or YouTube password.

Option 1: Disconnect a social account

  1. Sign in to BrightBean Studio.
  2. Open Social Accounts for the relevant workspace.
  3. Select the connected account and choose Disconnect or Remove.
  4. For additional control, open the connected platform’s account settings and revoke BrightBean Studio / Social Studio access.
  5. For Google or YouTube, you can also revoke access from Google's third-party connections settings.

Disconnecting stops new API access for that account and removes the active authorization from BrightBean Studio. Content already published to the social platform remains there until you remove it on that platform.

For YouTube, disconnecting in BrightBean Studio triggers token revocation and immediately deletes the connected-channel record, stored YouTube API profile data, publishing records tied to that connection, and associated analytics snapshots. Revoking access in Google settings stops new access; after BrightBean Studio detects that authorization can no longer be verified, associated YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days.

Option 2: Delete your BrightBean Studio account

If your account settings show a Delete account action, you can submit deletion there. Workspace owners may first need to transfer ownership or delete the organization to avoid removing data belonging to other team members.

Option 3: Send a deletion request

Email admin@shopauth.cloud with the subject “BrightBean Studio data deletion request” and include:

  • The email address used for your BrightBean Studio account.
  • The organization or workspace name, if applicable.
  • The username or channel handle of any connected Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or other social account to be removed.
  • Whether you want one integration removed or the entire BrightBean Studio account deleted.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, recovery codes, or other secrets. We may ask for a reasonable identity-verification step before deleting data.

What happens next

  • We will acknowledge a valid request within 7 days.
  • We will complete deletion within 30 days unless a legal or security exception applies.
  • OAuth credentials and active social-platform access will be revoked or removed.
  • Account profile data, workspace data you solely control, drafts, media, schedules, and stored platform data covered by the request will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized.
  • Shared workspace content may remain under another authorized owner where deletion is not legally required.

Backups and required retention

Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups and limited security logs for up to 90 days before automatic rotation. We may retain the minimum data necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or document completion of the request. Retained data is not used for normal product features.

Removing data from a connected platform

Deleting data from BrightBean Studio does not automatically delete posts, messages, or account data held independently by Meta, Instagram, Google, YouTube, or another platform. Use the relevant platform’s controls to delete platform-hosted content or the social account itself.
