This policy explains how Piotr Kwiatkowski, operating this BrightBean Social Studio deployment under the ShopAuth Cloud service name (“BrightBean Studio”, “we”, “us”), handles personal data when you use studio.shopauth.cloud, connect social-media accounts, publish content, or use messaging and analytics features. We do not sell personal data.

1. Who controls your data

The data controller for this deployment is Piotr Kwiatkowski, an individual operating the ShopAuth Cloud service, at ul. Ludowa 9A, 05-816 Michałowice, Mazowieckie, Poland. Privacy and data-protection requests can be sent to admin@shopauth.cloud.

2. Data we collect

Account and workspace data

  • Your email address, display name, profile image, authentication records, and account preferences.
  • Organizations, workspaces, team memberships, roles, approval records, and audit history.
  • Billing contact, selected plan, subscription status, and payment-processor customer or checkout identifiers. Payment-card details are entered directly with Stripe and are not stored by BrightBean Studio.
  • Security and technical data such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, logs, and error reports.

Connected social-platform data

When you connect Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or another supported platform, we receive only the data permitted by the scopes you approve and made available by that platform’s API. Depending on the features you enable, this may include:

  • Account and professional-profile identifiers, username, profile image, and account metadata.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to act on the connected account.
  • Posts, Reels, media metadata, captions, publishing status, and performance insights.
  • Comments, mentions, conversations, messages, sender identifiers, and webhook events.
  • Page or business-account relationships required to identify assets you manage.

BrightBean Studio does not ask for your Instagram, Facebook, Google, or YouTube password. Authentication takes place on the platform’s own authorization screen.

Content you provide

We process drafts, captions, uploaded images and videos, schedules, content ideas, approval comments, automation rules, and other materials you submit. If you enable AI-assisted features, the relevant prompt and content may be sent to the AI provider configured for your workspace.

Cookies and similar technologies

BrightBean Studio uses first-party session, security, preference, and CSRF cookies that are necessary to sign you in, protect requests, and keep the service working. We do not use Google or YouTube API data for advertising cookies or cross-site advertising profiles.

3. Why we use data

  • To create and secure your account and provide workspaces and collaboration features.
  • To connect social accounts and perform actions you request, including publishing, scheduling, analytics, comment management, and messaging.
  • To receive webhook events and run automation rules configured by authorized users.
  • To provide the free service tier and administer paid monthly subscriptions, checkout, billing status, and cancellations.
  • To provide support, detect abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you, your consent for optional integrations, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and compliance with legal obligations. You can withdraw an integration permission by disconnecting the account or revoking access on the social platform.

4. How we share data

We disclose data only where necessary to operate the service:

  • Connected platforms. We send content and instructions to Meta, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and other platforms only when you request or schedule an action.
  • Infrastructure providers. Hosting, storage, network, security, email, and monitoring providers process limited data on our behalf, including Hetzner and Cloudflare for this deployment.
  • Configured service providers. AI or media-processing providers receive only the content needed for a feature you choose to use.
  • Payment processor. Stripe processes checkout and subscription payments and receives the billing and transaction information needed to provide those services.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose data when required by law or necessary to protect users, the public, our rights, or the security of the service.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use private messages for advertising.

5. Meta and Instagram Platform Data

Data received from Meta products is used only to provide user-facing functionality authorized by the connected account, such as publishing content, displaying analytics, managing comments, and handling messages. Our use and transfer of that data is subject to the applicable Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Access is limited to authorized workspace members and service components that need it.

6. Google and YouTube API Services data

When you connect a YouTube channel, BrightBean Studio uses Google OAuth and the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. Depending on the features you authorize, we access and use:

  • Your YouTube channel ID, channel name, handle, profile image, subscriber count, view count, and video count so you can identify and manage the correct channel.
  • Videos or Shorts you choose to upload, plus the title, description, tags, category, made-for-kids setting, visibility setting, upload status, and YouTube video ID needed to complete and display the publishing action you requested.
  • Comment threads and replies when you choose to view, post, or reply to comments through the service.
  • Channel and video analytics, such as views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers, likes, comments, and shares, when analytics access is enabled.
  • Encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens used to perform authorized requests. We never receive or store your Google password.

We use this data only to provide the user-facing YouTube features you select: connecting the channel, publishing or scheduling content, showing publishing status, displaying comments and posting user-requested comments or replies, and presenting analytics to the channel owner or authorized workspace members. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, use it to train generalized AI models, or disclose it to third parties except service providers acting for us or Google/YouTube as necessary to carry out your request.

BrightBean Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

You can revoke BrightBean Studio's access in the product by disconnecting the channel, or at any time from Google's third-party connections settings.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep account and workspace data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you disconnect a social account, its active authorization is removed and the service stops requesting new platform data for that account. Valid account-deletion requests are completed within 30 days unless data must be retained for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting and tax requirements, or another legal obligation. Subscription and transaction records may therefore be retained for the legally required period after account closure, while payment-card data remains with the payment processor.

YouTube data retention

  • We verify connected YouTube authorization regularly (normally every six hours). Channel profile metadata is refreshed during those checks and not kept stale beyond 30 days while authorization remains valid.
  • We keep the YouTube video ID and the publication settings you selected as part of your publishing history. We do not retain the full raw API upload response.
  • Historical analytics may be retained while the YouTube connection remains authorized and the data is needed to provide analytics requested by the channel owner. Authorization is reconfirmed at least every 30 days.
  • Disconnecting a YouTube channel in BrightBean Studio triggers token revocation and deletes the connected-channel record and its stored YouTube API data immediately. If Google revokes access outside the service and we can no longer verify authorization, we stop new access and delete the associated YouTube API data no later than 30 days after detecting the invalid authorization.

Limited security logs and encrypted backups may remain for up to 90 days before automatic rotation. Data retained for legal or security purposes is isolated from normal product use. Detailed deletion instructions are available on our Data Deletion page.

8. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, restricted administrative access, credential protection, backups, logging, and other technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. No online service can guarantee absolute security; please notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. International transfers

Service providers or connected platforms may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms and contractual safeguards. Each connected platform also processes data under its own privacy policy.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We may ask you to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Children

BrightBean Studio is a business service and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. Material changes will be announced in the service or by another reasonable method. The effective date above identifies the current version.
